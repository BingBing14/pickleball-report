lead: Overall, we think our tournament went well. It started messy, got better quickly, and finished on time.

What went well

  • All 12 players showed up and all 8 matches were completed.
  • We finished in about 40 minutes (1:15 PM to about 1:55 PM), well inside our one-hour block.
  • The 6-minute cap and first-to-7 rule kept every round moving, and both courts were in use every round.
  • Once everyone understood their role, the tournament ran smoothly.
  • Calling the score before every serve kept the players and scorekeepers in agreement.

What we would improve

  • Have scorecards so players can see the score.
  • Prepare awards so the tournament has a proper ending.
  • Make the Final longer than the other matches.
  • Go over everyone's role before the opening, not during the first matches.

What we learned

  • How to work together by doing our roles. The tournament only worked because each of the 15 of us did one job and trusted the others to do theirs.
  • Knowing your role matters as much as the plan. Our plan was ready, but the messy start showed that everyone has to know exactly what to do before play begins.
  • Be ready to adjust on the day. Changing the match cap from 8 to 6 minutes on the day kept the tournament on time.
  • Small details shape the experience. Scorecards and awards seem minor, but missing them affected how the matches felt and how the event ended.

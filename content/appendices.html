Appendix E · Original score sheets and standings sheet

Photos of the sheets our officials and tabulators used on the day. Select a sheet to enlarge it.

How these sheets compare with this report. Every match score on the sheets matches this report. Two differences:

  • M5 is written as 8–4 because play went one point past the target of 7. We record it as 7–4 (see Problems and Solutions).
  • The handwritten standings count wins and losses from every match, including the medal round. The standings in this report count pool matches only, as the format requires. The final rankings are the same either way.

The printed times on Sheet 2 (8-minute matches and 3-minute changeovers) were our first plan. On the day we used a 6-minute cap and first to 7 points (see Schedule / Timeline).

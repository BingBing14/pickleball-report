lead: The tournament started messy but got better as it went on. These are the problems we ran into, what we did about them, and what we would do next time.

  1. Some members were unsure of their roles at the start

    The first matches were messy because some of us were lost and did not know exactly what our role was.

    What we did

    We figured out our roles quickly during the first matches, and the tournament ran more smoothly from then on.

    Next time

    Walk through every role, on the court, before the opening.

  2. No scorecards to show the score

    We did not have scorecards, so players could not see the score.

    What we did

    The referee called the score out loud before every serve, so players and scorekeepers always knew it.

    Next time

    Bring a flip scorecard or a simple score display for each court.

  3. One match went past the target score

    In M5 (A2 vs A3), play continued to 8 points even though games were to 7.

    What we did

    The result was recorded as 7–4, the score when A2 reached the target. The winner did not change.

    Next time

    The scorekeeper announces game point so the referee stops play at 7.

  4. No awards, so the tournament ended abruptly

    We forgot to prepare awards, so there was no proper closing for the winners.

    What we did

    The results were announced, and the courts were handed over to Group 2.

    Next time

    Prepare simple certificates or medals and plan a short awarding at the end.

  5. The Final was short

    With the same 6-minute, first-to-7 rule as every other match, the Final ended 7–1 quickly.

    What we did

    We kept the same rule for every match so the tournament stayed on time.

    Next time

    Give the Final more time or a higher target, since we finished with time to spare.

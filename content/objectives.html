Course objectives

From the instructor's tournament guidelines, students should be able to:

  1. Plan and organize a pickleball tournament

    What we did

    We split our 15 members into 8 committees, drew the 12 players into 6 doubles teams, set up two round-robin pools with a medal round, and printed score sheets for the officials.

    Where to see it

    Committees, Format, Schedule

  2. Apply proper pickleball rules and officiating procedures

    What we did

    We used the standard doubles rules with two time-saving changes (games to 7, a 6-minute cap). Each court had a referee and a scorekeeper, and a rules marshal moved between both courts.

    Where to see it

    Rules and Regulations, Officiating committee

  3. Demonstrate teamwork, leadership and communication

    What we did

    Our group leader, {{leader}}, coordinated the group and officiated. Our Tournament Director, {{director}}, ran the event and announced results. The call desk told each pair when they were up next.

    Where to see it

    Committees, Reflection

  4. Manage time, equipment, scores and participants

    What we did

    All 8 matches were played between {{site.start}} and {{site.end}}. Officials kept score on the sheets, and tabulators turned them into pool standings that decided the medal round.

    Where to see it

    Schedule, Results, Equipment

  5. Demonstrate sportsmanship and safety

    What we did

    All 12 players showed up and every match was completed. Our safety officer checked the courts before play and watched the matches.

    Where to see it

    Safety / First Aid, Photos

  6. Evaluate the success of the tournament

    What we did

    After the tournament we listed what went wrong, what we did about it, and what we would change next time.

    Where to see it

    Problems and Solutions, Reflection

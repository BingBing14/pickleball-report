Plan vs. what happened

We first planned to start at 1:00 PM, with an 8-minute cap and 3-minute changeovers. Because some people were late, play only began at 1:15 PM, and we cut the matches to a 6-minute cap and first to 7 points, with shorter changeovers. That is how all 8 matches still fit, finishing at about 1:55 PM.

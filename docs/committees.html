Committees and Responsibilities

All 15 members of Group 1 had one role on the day, in one of 8 committees.

Group leader: Gionne Kalel Legaspi (also officiated). Tournament Director: Isabella Mae Arellano.

The four officials worked in pairs, one referee and one scorekeeper per court, and swapped jobs after Round 1 so that each of them did both.

Tournament Director · 1 member

  • Isabella Mae Arellano

Responsibilities

Tournament Format / System

Six doubles teams played two round-robin pools of three, one pool per court, then a Final and a third-place match.

Why we chose this format

We had 2 courts, 12 players and about one hour. Two pools of three meant both courts were in use every round, every team played at least two matches, and the whole tournament fit into four rounds.

1. Team draw

The 12 players of Group 2 were drawn at random into 6 doubles pairs. Teams A1 to A3 formed Pool A (Court 1) and teams B1 to B3 formed Pool B (Court 2).

2. Pool play (Rounds 1–3)

Each team played the other two teams in its pool once. Pools were ranked by:

  1. Number of wins
  2. Head-to-head result
  3. Point differential (points won minus points lost)
  4. Fewest points allowed
  5. Coin flip

3. Medal round (Round 4)

  • Final (Court 1): Pool A 1st vs Pool B 1st
  • Third-place match (Court 2): Pool A 2nd vs Pool B 2nd
  • The two teams that finished 3rd in their pools were placed 5th and 6th by wins, then point differential.

4. Match length

Every match was one game to 7 points with a 6-minute cap. If neither team reached 7 before time ran out, the team ahead won. Our first plan was an 8-minute cap with 3-minute changeovers, but we shortened it on the day to keep the tournament on time. See Rules and Regulations for all the rules we used.

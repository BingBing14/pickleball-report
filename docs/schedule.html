Section 05 of 12

Schedule / Timeline

We planned to start at 1:00 PM, but play began at 1:15 PM. All 8 matches were done by about 1:55 PM, in four rounds of two matches.

Timeline of our block

  1. 1:00 PM · Planned start

    Some people were late, so we waited. While waiting, we were still changing the rules and match length for the tournament.

  2. 1:15 PM · Opening

    The Tournament Director opened the event and the first two matches were called to Courts 1 and 2.

  3. Round 1 · Pool play

    M1 on Court 1 (Pool A) and M2 on Court 2 (Pool B).

  4. Round 2 · Pool play

    M3 and M4. The officials swapped referee and scorekeeper jobs.

  5. Round 3 · Pool play

    M5 and M6. After this round the tabulators finished the pool standings.

  6. Round 4 · Medal round

    The Final (M7) on Court 1 and the third-place match (M8) on Court 2.

  7. About 1:55 PM · Finish

    Results were announced and the courts were handed over to Group 2, who then ran a bracket for our players.

Time per match

Match cap
6 minutes, or the first team to 7 points
Changeover
About 1–2 minutes between rounds
Rounds
4 (two matches at a time, one per court)
Total
About 40 minutes of play, 1:15 PM to about 1:55 PM

Plan vs. what happened

We first planned to start at 1:00 PM, with an 8-minute cap and 3-minute changeovers. Because some people were late, play only began at 1:15 PM, and we cut the matches to a 6-minute cap and first to 7 points, with shorter changeovers. That is how all 8 matches still fit, finishing at about 1:55 PM.

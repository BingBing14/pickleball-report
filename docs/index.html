Introduction

This website is the Tournament Report of Group 1, section 2FSM-4, for SW2928 Individual and Dual Sports. As our capstone, the class planned and ran a pickleball tournament. This report covers how we planned it, how it ran on the day and how we evaluated it.

The class was split into two groups. First, we (Group 1, team color White) ran the bracket while Group 2's players competed. Then the groups switched roles: Group 2 ran a bracket and our members played in it. This report is about the tournament we organized.

Six doubles teams played two round-robin pools on two courts, followed by a Final and a third-place match. Play started at 1:15 PM and finished at about 1:55 PM, inside our block of about one hour.
